## Security policy

Found a flaw in crivo? Use GitHub's private reporting (the **Security** tab, **Report a
vulnerability**) or write to junioad555@gmail.com with what you found and how to reproduce it. Do
not open a public issue with exploitable details.

What crivo does and does not guarantee is in the [README](README.en.md), under "crivo's own
security".
